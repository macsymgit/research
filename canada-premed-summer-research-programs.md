## 1. Executive Summary

### TL;DR for parents

- **Canada has no NIH-style central catalogue.** Most Canadian summer research is **supervisor-matched**: the student finds a professor first, and the money (an NSERC USRA, a hospital studentship or a charity award) follows the student–supervisor pair. A US-style "list of programs to apply to" covers only part of the landscape. The cold email to a professor *is* the application.
- **The best-paid, most accessible option is at home.** The **NSERC USRA at McMaster** pays **$6,000 (NSERC) + at least $3,268 (McMaster Science top-up) ≈ $9,268 for 14–16 weeks**. It is open after one completed year to citizens/PR with a cumulative GPA ≥ 9.0 (on McMaster's 12-point scale), and department deadlines run **Feb 2–28** ([our.science.mcmaster.ca](https://our.science.mcmaster.ca/funding-2/nserc-usra/), [nserc-crsng.canada.ca](https://nserc-crsng.canada.ca/en/funding-opportunity/undergraduate-student-research-awards)).
- **Most of the US programs in the original post are closed to Canadians.** NIH SIP, Fred Hutch, St. Jude, Mayo SURF, Amgen US and Stanford SURF require US citizenship/PR or enrolment at a US school. A few elite US programs do take Canadians (**Rockefeller SURF** explicitly; JAX and CSHL are unclear).
- **Housing is almost never provided in Canada.** For a Hamilton-based student, the practical radius is Hamilton plus Toronto (commutable). Out-of-province programs mean paying rent from the stipend.

### TL;DR for students

1. **Your first summer (2027) is constrained by year of study.** Several flagship programs (Lunenfeld, Dalhousie, Amgen Canada, DAAD RISE, Mitacs Globalink, OIST, Mayo, St. Jude) require **two or more completed years**. Section 3 lists what is open to you after Year 1.
2. **Start emailing professors in late November to January, not in February.** By the time McMaster USRA deadlines arrive (Feb 2027), supervisors have usually already picked their students.
3. **Your Fall 2026 grades are your whole academic record** for 2027 applications. A ≥ 9.0 cumulative GPA by December unlocks the McMaster USRA.

### Three things most Canadian premeds get wrong about summer research

1. **They wait for a posting.** In Canada most positions are never posted. They are created when a supervisor agrees to take you and then applies for (or tops up) funding. If you wait for an ad, you are competing only for the minority of positions that are posted (no source quantifies the split; treat this as a qualitative pattern).
2. **They over-value prestige over continuity.** Medical school files reward **sustained depth** (one lab, 2+ years, a poster or paper) more than a different famous institute each summer. A McMaster lab you can continue in during Year 2 and Year 3 terms is usually worth more than a one-off Toronto summer. See [the plan doc's Year 2–3 research section](mcmaster-life-sci-to-med-school-plan.md).
3. **They ignore the year-of-study gate.** Applying to Lunenfeld or Amgen Canada after Year 1 wastes the reference letters you would have saved for programs you qualify for. Read the "Min. year" column first.

## 2. How Canada Differs From the US Model

The original post's five fields (who can apply, citizenship, funding, dates, link) assume the US structure: a national menu of standalone SURF-type programs with cohorts, housing and a central portal. Canada works differently.

| Dimension | US model (original post) | Canadian reality | What this means for you |
|---|---|---|---|
| **How you apply** | Central portal; program assigns you to a lab | **Mostly supervisor-first**: secure a professor, then a joint student + supervisor application (USRA, hospital studentships, charity awards). Central portals exist only at Lunenfeld, Women's College, Holland Bloorview, CHU Sainte-Justine, Amgen Canada | Your cold-email list *is* your application list |
| **Who can apply** | Many national SURFs are open to any US undergrad | **Federal USRA can only be held where the offer is made**, and many university programs are **home-institution-only** (Queen's USSRF, Western Schulich, uOttawa MD, UBC FoM, Memorial) | Your home university (McMaster) is your highest-probability venue |
| **Citizenship** | US citizen / green card; F-1 rules vary | **USRA requires citizen/PR/Protected Person.** Hospital programs usually only need **legal authorization to work in Canada** (e.g., Women's College, Unity Health) | A domestic student faces almost no citizenship barrier in Canada |
| **Funding** | Stipend plus housing plus travel is common | **Stipend or hourly wage only.** Housing/travel is essentially never covered (CHU Sainte-Justine and Unity Health say so explicitly) | Out-of-town programs cost you rent. Hamilton plus Toronto is the efficient radius |
| **Timeline** | Dec–Feb deadlines | **Similar (Jan–Feb), but the supervisor match must happen first**, so the real start is Nov–Jan | Begin outreach in late November 2026 |
| **Year gate** | Most SURFs: rising sophomore or later | Mixed. Some explicitly welcome first-years (SickKids); others require 2+ completed years (Lunenfeld, Dalhousie) | Section 3 filter |

> *Bottom line: in Canada the "program" is often just the funding wrapper. The scarce resource is a supervisor who says yes, so treat the directory below as a map of **where funded supervisors are**, not a list of forms to submit.*

## 11. How to Land a Supervisor (the Real Application)

The original post focused on finding programs. In Canada, the bottleneck is the supervisor. This approach works:

1. **Targeting.** Pick professors whose labs have (a) current funding (an NSERC Discovery or CIHR grant, visible on their faculty page or in recent papers), (b) a history of undergraduate co-authors, and (c) work you can explain in one sentence. McMaster first: Biology, Biochemistry & Biomedical Sciences, PNB, Kinesiology, Health Research Methods (PHRI), and the IIDR/Farncombe/MIRA institute labs.
2. **The email (≤ 150 words).** Subject: "Summer 2027 research: Year 1 Life Sci student (USRA-eligible)". One sentence on a specific recent paper. One sentence on why (a skill or course link). One sentence of logistics: "I'm eligible for NSERC USRA (citizen, GPA X) and available May–Aug 2027." Attach a 1-page CV and an unofficial transcript.
3. **Volume and timing.** Send in waves of 5–8, two weeks apart, starting late November. Follow up once after 10 days. A typical reply rate is low, so plan for 15–25 emails to get 2–4 conversations (treat as an approximate planning range, not a sourced statistic).
4. **The meeting.** Ask what a summer student did last year, who supervises day to day (often a grad student), and whether there is a path to an independent-study or thesis project in later years.
5. **Close.** Once a professor says yes, ask which funding they want you to apply for (USRA, an institute studentship or their grant) and diarize that deadline immediately.

| **2029 (after Y3, before OMSAS submission in Oct 2029)** | Same lab → 4th-year thesis; or Mitacs Globalink / OIST if continuity is already established | A first-author abstract or co-authored paper; a strong research reference letter |

The plan doc targets **2+ years, ~600 research hours and one poster** before OMSAS ([mcmaster-life-sci-to-med-school-plan.md](mcmaster-life-sci-to-med-school-plan.md)). Three summers in one lab (~14 weeks × 35 h ≈ 490 h each) clear that bar comfortably. Three summers in three different labs often do not produce the poster or the reference letter.
