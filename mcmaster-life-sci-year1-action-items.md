## Why This Matters (the core problem)

| Risk if left at 18 units | Explanation |
|---|---|
| **Incomplete Level I** | Level II program selection assumes a completed 30-unit Level I with Gateway prerequisites done. |
| **Delayed graduation** | Honours BSc = 120 units. At 18/year you'd need ~6+ years instead of 4. |
| **Lost scholarships** | Renewable McMaster entrance scholarships generally require a **30-unit full load**; 18 units can forfeit them. (9 units/term keeps "full-time" status only.) |

**Net effect:** Adding the 4 courses takes you from **18 → 30 units**, completing the full official Gateway year and keeping every Level II / premed door open.
